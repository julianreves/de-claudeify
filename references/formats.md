# Other formats

The scanner reads web files. For everything else the same rules apply, checked by hand. Run this list before handing back a Word, PowerPoint, PDF, spreadsheet or email.

## All formats

- One typeface. Set it in the styles or the theme, not on individual runs.
- No decorative lines: none under titles, around boxes or on every table cell. A line that separates ideas or sections, or carries data, is fine.
- No subtitle, eyebrow or kicker made of terms joined by dots, bullets, slashes or pipes.
- No emoji, filler icons, badges, gradients, drop shadows or ornamental callout boxes.
- Restrained color: mostly black, white and grey, with one or two accents that mean something.
- Titles are sentence case and make a claim.
- One idea per page or slide. Generous whitespace.
- Every number has a source.
- No em dashes. No "not X, but Y". No stock marketing words.

## Word (.docx)

- Set the font in the Normal style and the heading styles.
- Remove paragraph borders and the horizontal-line autoformat.
- Tables: no borders on every cell. Align on tab stops or use a borderless table with row spacing. A total row may have a top border.
- Remove shaded callout paragraphs. Write a normal paragraph.
- Headers and footers carry a page number at most.

## PowerPoint (.pptx)

- Set the font in the theme (major and minor fonts), not per run.
- One claim per slide as the title. No label above the title.
- No shapes with outlines used as cards. Group with space.
- Remove shadows from shapes and text (the default shape style adds them).
- No gradient fills. Surface is plain white or plain black.
- Charts: label bars directly, no legend, no gridlines.

## PDF

Fix the source (Word, HTML, slides) and export again. Do not edit a PDF to remove tells.

## Spreadsheets

- One font. No fills on header rows beyond one neutral tone. No borders on every cell.
- Number formats are consistent. Units in the header. Source cell for imported data.

## Email and chat

- No emoji headers, no bold lead-ins on every paragraph, no bullet lists of three where a sentence does it.
- No "I hope this finds you well", no closing offer to "reach out with any questions".
