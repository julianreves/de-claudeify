# Writing like a person

The scanner catches the stock phrases. This file covers the rest.

## Rules

- Write full sentences, the way you would say them out loud. A subject and a verb. "Our three wines come from the same forty-one rows" is a sentence. "Three wines, all from the same forty-one rows" is a fragment with a tail, and models write it constantly. The same goes for stacked short sentences: "128 orders this week. Two are late." becomes "We shipped 128 orders this week, and two of them are late." Read every headline aloud. If nobody would say it, rewrite it.
- Clear and direct, and vary sentence length. A short sentence is fine when it is a real sentence.
- Say what happens, with the nouns and verbs a customer would use. "Send an invoice and see when it is paid" beats "Streamline your billing workflow".
- State a point once, in the form you mean. No "It's not X, it's Y". No "not just X but Y". No two-beat pattern where a claim is followed by a sentence starting with "But" that flips it. The same applies in Spanish ("no es X, es Y", "no solo X sino Y").
- No em dashes. Use a comma, a period or parentheses. In deck text, avoid hyphens and dashes altogether.
- Headings are sentence case and make a claim. "Your books are closed by the fifth", not "Our Process".
- No closing wrap-up ("In conclusion", "Ready to get started?"). End on the last real thing.
- No forced groups of three. Use as many items as there are.
- First person when writing as the person or company ("we close your books"). Formal but human.
- Match the language of the audience. Spanish for a Mexican audience is Mexican Spanish.

## Proof

- Never invent a metric, a testimonial, a logo, a client or a count of users.
- A number has a source and a date. An illustrative number is labelled illustrative.
- A quote in quotation marks is verbatim and has a name.
- If a fact is missing, leave one visible placeholder such as `[[CLIENT: real figure and source]]`.

## Stock words the scanner warns about

seamless, elevate, unlock, empower, streamline, revolutionize, cutting-edge, game-changer, supercharge, effortless, fast-paced, take it to the next level, delve, leverage, holistic, next-generation, state-of-the-art, world-class, best-in-class, ready to get started, everything you need, tapestry, digital landscape, one-stop shop, mission-critical, meticulous, whether you're, robust.

Spanish: de vanguardia, soluciones integrales, al siguiente nivel, sin complicaciones.

A WARN is a prompt to look, not a ban. "Robust" is fine in a materials specification. Waive it with a reason when it is the right word.

| Three wines, all from the same forty-one rows | Our three wines come from the same forty-one rows |
| 128 orders this week. Two are late. | We shipped 128 orders this week, and two of them are late. |
| One hillside, picked by hand. | We pick our grapes by hand on a single hillside. |
| Unlock seamless financial workflows | Send an invoice and see when it is paid |
| Empower your team to do more | Everyone sees the same numbers |
| Built for the modern business | Built for shops with fewer than 20 people (if true) |
| It's not accounting, it's peace of mind | We close your books by the fifth |
| Trusted by 10,000+ teams | (remove, or a real client with permission) |
| Ready to get started? | Book a call (as the one plain link, where it belongs) |
